Data caveats

This is a prototype, not a verified current infrastructure map. The Network Rail GIS archive is explicitly marked out of date. Track geometry represents modelled centreline links; it is not an engineering or safety-critical representation.

The map uses the supplied Greater London boundary; archived route and region polygons and track links are clipped to it. The River Thames is requested from the public GLA GIS service in verified feature-ID batches. It requires network access; the application refuses incomplete batches rather than showing a truncated river. Its status is displayed above. Station markers are approximate station-complex positions. Leaflet is loaded online; no third-party basemap tiles are requested.
